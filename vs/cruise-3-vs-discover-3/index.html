OHM Cruise 3 CA$3,499

Full OHM Cruise 3 specifications

The comfort-first step-through: an upright position, a Shimano E7000 system with 60 Nm, and a maker that has designed in North Vancouver since 2005 and states a 10-year parts guarantee. It is also the dearest bike on this page.

Velotric Discover 3 CA$2,699

Full Velotric Discover 3 specifications

The all-round daily commuter: a 750 W motor, a 730 Wh battery and the second-longest claimed range here (~129 km of pedal assist), with lights, fenders and a rear rack included. It ships as Class 2; Class 3 (28 mph) is unlocked in the app where local rules allow.
