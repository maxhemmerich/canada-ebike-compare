Which one, in one line each

Match the bike to your ride, not to the biggest number on the box.

Apartment, stairs, or carrying it onto transit

Velotric Tempo · CA$2,099

At 39 lb the Tempo is 16 lb lighter than anything else here, and light enough to ride with the motor off. The 374 Wh battery is the trade-off.

The longest commute on one charge

Surface 604 Rook · CA$2,699

The Rook carries the biggest battery here (960 Wh) and claims the longest range of the five: up to 150 km in eco. It is also the only bike here that ships at Class 3.

The best all-round daily commuter

Velotric Discover 3 · CA$2,699

750 W of motor, a 730 Wh battery and ~129 km of claimed pedal assist, with lights, fenders and a rack included. The most complete package of the five.

The cheapest way in, without buying a toy

Rad Power Bikes RadKick 7-Speed · CA$1,899

At CA$1,899 the RadKick 7-Speed is the lowest price here and still has hydraulic brakes and a rear rack. Its 360 Wh battery is the honest limit: short trips only.

A bike that feels like a bike, on a long mixed commute

Rad Power Bikes Radster Road · CA$2,699

The Radster Road pairs a torque sensor with a 100 Nm motor, so the assist follows your pedalling instead of pushing you. It is the heaviest of the five.

Which one for which household

The decision guide, in full. The same pages are in the free PDF.

Rad Power Bikes Radster Road CA$2,699

A long, bike-feeling commute: the torque sensor and 100 Nm motor make pedalling feel assisted rather than motorised. It is the heaviest bike here, so it suits a garage or ground-floor lock-up.

Source: Rad Power Bikes product page, checked 2026-10-07.

Rad Power Bikes RadKick 7-Speed CA$1,899

Short trips and stairs: Rad's lightest bike at 55 lb, with a rear rack rated 55 lb. The small 360 Wh battery is the trade-off - it is the shortest-range bike in this set.

Source: Rad Power Bikes product page, checked 2026-10-07.

Velotric Tempo CA$2,099

Carrying-up-stairs and mixed bike/motor use: at 39 lb it is by far the lightest here, and light enough to ride with the motor off. The 374 Wh battery is the smallest, so plan short hops or charge often.

Source: Velotric product page, checked 2026-10-07.

Velotric Discover 3 CA$2,699

The all-round daily commuter: a 750 W motor, a 730 Wh battery and the second-longest claimed range here (~129 km of pedal assist), with lights, fenders and a rear rack included. It ships as Class 2; Class 3 (28 mph) is unlocked in the app where local rules allow.

Source: Velotric product page, checked 2026-10-07.

Surface 604 Rook CA$2,699

The long-haul, high-speed pick: the biggest battery here (960 Wh) and the only bike in this set that ships at Class 3, with a 45 km/h claimed top speed. Vancouver-designed, with a 5-year frame warranty. It was Out of Stock when checked, and its motor wattage and weight are not published on the product page.

Source: Surface 604 product page, checked 2026-10-07.
