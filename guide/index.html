Which one, in one line each

Match the bike to your ride, not to the biggest number on the box. These are the same one-line verdicts the comparison page and the PDF carry, from the same list.

Apartment, stairs, or carrying it onto transit

Velotric Tempo · CA$2,099

At 39 lb the Tempo is the lightest bike here - 7 lb under the next lightest - and light enough to ride with the motor off. The 374 Wh battery is the trade-off.

The cheapest way in, without buying a toy

Aventon Soltera 2.5 · CA$1,699

Limited stock when checked

At CA$1,699 the Soltera 2.5 is the lowest price here and still a real bike: Tektro hydraulic disc brakes, a torque sensor and lights front and rear, UL 2849 / UL 2271 certified. Its 345.6 Wh battery is the honest limit: short, light trips.

The longest commute on one charge

Surface 604 Rook · CA$2,699

Out of stock when checked

The Rook carries the biggest battery here (960 Wh) and, at up to 150 km in eco, claims the joint-longest range on this page - the same figure OHM publishes for the Cruise 3. It is also the only bike here that ships at Class 3.

The best all-round daily commuter

Velotric Discover 3 · CA$2,699

750 W is the highest motor rating in this set, and its 730 Wh battery is the second biggest - behind only the Rook's 960 Wh - with lights, fenders and a rack included.

The lowest-priced Rad Power bike here

Rad Power Bikes RadKick 7-Speed · CA$1,899

At CA$1,899 the RadKick 7-Speed still has hydraulic brakes and a rear rack included, and it is the lightest Rad on this page. Its 40 Nm is the least torque here and its 360 Wh battery is the honest limit: short trips only.

A bike that feels like a bike, on a long mixed commute

Rad Power Bikes Radster Road · CA$2,699

The Radster Road pairs a torque sensor with a 100 Nm motor, so the assist follows your pedalling instead of pushing you. It is the heaviest bike here.

The premium step-through commuter

OHM Cruise 3 · CA$3,499

In stock when checked

CA$3,499 buys a Shimano E7000 system with 60 Nm, a 504 Wh in-tube battery, a 2-year warranty and 30-day returns, from a maker that has designed in North Vancouver since 2005. The dearest bike here; the maker sells it on comfort rather than speed.

Which one for which household

Which bike suits which rider, one paragraph each, the same text the PDF carries.

Aventon Soltera 2.5 CA$1,699

Limited stock when checked

A flat-city commuter on the lowest budget here: at 46 lb it is light, and it still carries hydraulic disc brakes, a torque sensor and front and rear lights. The 345.6 Wh battery and the maker's own Eco-mode range figure suit short, light trips rather than long ones.

Source: Aventon product page, checked 2026-10-09.

Rad Power Bikes RadKick 7-Speed CA$1,899

Short trips and stairs: Rad's lightest bike at 55 lb, with a rear rack rated 55 lb. The small 360 Wh battery is the trade-off - it is the shortest-range bike in this set.

Source: Rad Power Bikes product page, checked 2026-10-09.

Velotric Tempo CA$2,099

Carrying-up-stairs and mixed bike/motor use: at 39 lb it is by far the lightest here, and light enough to ride with the motor off. The 374 Wh battery is the smallest, so plan short hops or charge often.

Source: Velotric product page, checked 2026-10-09.

Velotric Discover 3 CA$2,699

The all-round daily commuter: a 750 W motor, a 730 Wh battery and the second-longest claimed range here (~129 km of pedal assist), with lights, fenders and a rear rack included. It ships as Class 2; Class 3 (28 mph) is unlocked in the app where local rules allow.

Source: Velotric product page, checked 2026-10-09.

Rad Power Bikes Radster Road CA$2,699

A long, bike-feeling commute: the torque sensor and 100 Nm motor make pedalling feel assisted rather than motorised. It is the heaviest bike here, so it suits a garage or ground-floor lock-up.

Source: Rad Power Bikes product page, checked 2026-10-09.

Surface 604 Rook CA$2,699

Out of stock when checked

The long-haul, high-speed pick: the biggest battery here (960 Wh) and the only bike in this set that ships at Class 3, with a 45 km/h claimed top speed. Vancouver-designed, with a 5-year frame warranty. It was Out of Stock when checked.

Source: Surface 604 product page, checked 2026-10-09.

OHM Cruise 3 CA$3,499

In stock when checked

The comfort-first step-through: an upright position, a Shimano E7000 system with 60 Nm, and a maker that has designed in North Vancouver since 2005 and states a 10-year parts guarantee. It is also the dearest bike on this page.

Source: OHM product page, checked 2026-10-09.
