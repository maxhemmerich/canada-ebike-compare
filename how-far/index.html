How this page decides

  1. It only ever quotes the maker. Each top figure and each lowest published figure above is a number the maker itself published, quoted in full in the table, with a link to the page it was read from.
  2. Hills are not modelled. No maker here publishes a hills figure, so this page will not invent a percentage for you. The hills box changes which published figure you are measured against — the maker's own low end, where one exists.
  3. A caveat on the Rook. Surface 604 describes its published figure as eco mode, so that top figure is the maker's best case, not a worst case.
  4. Conversions, named. The Discover 3's lowest figure is published in miles (65 mi) and the Soltera 2.5's range is published in miles only (46 mi); both are shown here converted to kilometres at 1 mi = 1.609344 km.
  5. Where two of these figures come from. The Soltera 2.5's figure carries Aventon's own footnote — Eco mode, a 75 kg rider, flat paved road — so it is a best case as well. The Cruise 3's 100 km is stated in OHM's own product-page summary rather than in its specification table, and no assist level is named with it.
  6. Battery size is the whole story only sometimes. The Wh figure above is the maker's published capacity; two bikes can post the same miles from different Wh once weight and assist level are counted in.
